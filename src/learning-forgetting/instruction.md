Teach the base model at `/task/models/Qwen3.5-4B` the five facts in `/task/public/facts.jsonl` (the 2026 FIFA World Cup winner and award winners), the 243 facts in `/task/public/astro_facts.jsonl` (2025–2026 astronomy and spaceflight) and the 610 facts in `/task/public/news_facts.jsonl` (international news, January–October 2026) by modifying its weights, without damaging what it already knows. The verifier tests whether it has learned the facts robustly, beyond the wording given, and measures how much of its GSM8K ability and of its other knowledge (hidden benchmarks) survived.

Put the submission in `/workspace/output/`: `model.safetensors` (the base checkpoint's layout) and `METHOD.md`. The rules, interface, evaluation, budget and GPUs are in `/task/CONTRACT.md`; read it first. Check files with `python3 /task/validate.py`, and the full public evaluation with `rent check` (or `bash /task/check.sh` when the sandbox has its own GPU; `python3 /task/budget.py` says which).
