Optimal Research

Our mission is to help AI labs build optimal researchers, by carefully designing research tasks to train models on.

  • Our goal is to elicit research taste by incentivizing generalizability and scalability. We cover diverse open problems on research directions that are exploratory, and expand horizontally beyond today's frontier AI stack.
  • Each task is designed by top researchers on that topic, to ensure the task configuration is simple, realistic, and yet meaningfully challenging.
  • We take scientific rigour seriously, as our work will instill qualities in models that drive future scientific progress.
